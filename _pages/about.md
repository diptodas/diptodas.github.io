I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Bowers College of Computing and Information Science](https://bowers.cornell.edu/) at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), where I work with [Dr. Aditya Vashistha](https://www.adityavashistha.com/) as part of the [Cornell Global AI Initiative](https://globalai.ai.cornell.edu/). I study how AI systems and online platforms shape decision-making, public discourse, identity, and access to information.

My [research]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) centers on a recurring problem: algorithmic systems, and the mechanisms meant to hold them accountable, are often built on assumptions that do not hold where they are used. I study AI across the public sector, language technologies, and online platforms in low-resource and Global Majority contexts. I examine how AI is documented and governed, develop evaluation methods grounded in local context, and work with affected communities to build datasets and tools, combining computational audits with qualitative, critical, and participatory methods. This work appears in venues such as **CHI, CSCW, FAccT, AIES, DIS, GROUP, TOCHI, and USENIX Security**, has received four Best Paper Honorable Mention Awards, and has been [featured in the media]({{ '/media/' | relative_url }}), including The Atlantic and CBC. Before Cornell, I was a postdoctoral fellow in the [Department of Computer Science](https://web.cs.toronto.edu) at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), working with [Dr. Syed Ishtiaque Ahmed](https://www.ishtiaque.net/) and [Dr. Shion Guha](https://shionguha.ca/). I received my PhD and MS in Information Science from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/), where I worked with [Dr. Bryan Semaan](https://www.colorado.edu/faculty/semaan/). Earlier, I studied computer science at [Missouri State University](https://www.missouristate.edu/) (MS) and [Bangladesh University of Engineering &amp; Technology](http://www.buet.ac.bd/) (BSEngg).
