I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Bowers College of Computing and Information Science](https://bowers.cornell.edu/) at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), as part of the [Global AI Initiative](https://globalai.ai.cornell.edu/). My [research]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) focuses on responsible AI and its impact on society, especially for marginalized communities in Global Majority contexts and across cultures. I study how AI systems and online platforms shape decision-making, public discourse, identity, and access to information. These systems, and the policies meant to govern them, are often designed around assumptions that do not hold for the people they affect. I examine these gaps in three areas: AI governance in the public sector, the evaluation and alignment of AI systems, and online platforms and diaspora communities. Using both computational and human-centered methods, I build tools, datasets, and evidence that advance accountability and ethics in AI and make it useful for social good. Looking ahead, my work will move toward contestable AI in the public sector, participatory evaluation and repair of generative AI, and community-governed AI for fairness and justice. My research appears in top computing research venues such as **CHI, CSCW, FAccT, GROUP, DIS, AIES, TOCHI, and USENIX Security**, has received Best Paper Honorable Mention Awards and a recognition for diversity and inclusion, and has been [featured in the media]({{ '/media/' | relative_url }}), including The Atlantic and CBC.
