I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Bowers College of Computing and Information Science](https://bowers.cornell.edu/) at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), where I work with [Dr. Aditya Vashistha](https://www.adityavashistha.com/) as part of the [Cornell Global AI Initiative](https://globalai.ai.cornell.edu/). I study how AI systems and online platforms shape decision-making, public discourse, identity, and access to information.

My [research]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) focuses on responsible AI and its impact on society, especially for marginalized communities in Global Majority contexts and across cultures. AI systems, and the policies meant to govern them, are often designed around assumptions that do not hold for the people they affect. I study these gaps in three areas: AI governance in the public sector, the evaluation and alignment of AI systems, and online platforms and diaspora communities. Using both computational and human-centered methods, I build tools, datasets, and evidence that make AI more accountable and useful for social good. This work appears in venues such as **CHI, CSCW, FAccT, GROUP, DIS, AIES, TOCHI, and USENIX Security**, has received four Best Paper Honorable Mention Awards, and has been [featured in the media]({{ '/media/' | relative_url }}), including The Atlantic and CBC.
