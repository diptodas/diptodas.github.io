I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the [Bowers College of Computing and Information Science](https://bowers.cornell.edu/) at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), where I work with [Dr. Aditya Vashistha](https://www.adityavashistha.com/) as part of the [Cornell Global AI Initiative](https://globalai.ai.cornell.edu/). I study how AI systems and online platforms shape decision-making, public discourse, identity, and access to information.

My [research]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) centers on a recurring problem: algorithmic systems, and the mechanisms meant to hold them accountable, are often built on assumptions that do not hold where they are used. I focus especially on AI, studying it across the public sector, language technologies, and online platforms in low-resource and Global Majority contexts. A model can score well on standard benchmarks while still misrecognizing the identities of the people it claims to represent, and a government can make its algorithms visible without becoming accountable for them. To understand and close these gaps, I examine how AI is documented and governed, develop evaluation methods grounded in local context, and work with affected communities to build datasets and tools that let them shape how AI is assessed and repaired. Throughout, I combine computational audits and system building with qualitative, critical, and participatory methods. This work appears in venues such as **CHI, CSCW, FAccT, AIES, DIS, GROUP, TOCHI, and USENIX Security**, has received four Best Paper Honorable Mention Awards (most recently at CHI 2026 and DIS 2026), and has been featured in [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2024/01/quora-tragedy-answer-websites/677062/), [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/listen/live-radio/1-100-ottawa-morning/clip/16232262-the-federal-government-foreign-a.i.-companies), the [Ottawa Citizen](https://ottawacitizen.com/public-service/government-dependent-u-s-companies-public-service-ai), [Prothom Alo](https://www.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/8ml8jqzguh), and [Princeton University Press](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691255811/digital-culture-shock).

Before Cornell, I was a postdoctoral fellow in the [Department of Computer Science](https://web.cs.toronto.edu) at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), working with [Dr. Syed Ishtiaque Ahmed](https://www.ishtiaque.net/) and [Dr. Shion Guha](https://shionguha.ca/) with funding from the Institute of Health Emergencies and Pandemics. I received my PhD and MS in Information Science from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/), where I worked with [Dr. Bryan Semaan](https://www.colorado.edu/faculty/semaan/), an MS in Computer Science from [Missouri State University](https://www.missouristate.edu/), and a BSEngg in Computer Science and Engineering from [Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology](http://www.buet.ac.bd/). I have taught [seven courses]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}) as instructor of record at universities in the US, Canada, and Bangladesh, and received CU Boulder's Teaching Excellence Award in 2024.
