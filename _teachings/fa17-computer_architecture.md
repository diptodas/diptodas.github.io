## Course Overview

This course prepares students for work in microprocessor, embedded systems, and computer systems research by teaching the technical foundations of computer organization and architecture. I used lectures, demonstrations, presentations, and group exercises to connect quantitative performance principles with the design of real computer systems.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this course, students will be able to:

- Define and describe computer organization and architecture
- Classify and compare computer organizations, architectures, and their internal components
- Explain and apply how these components work together
- Analyze and design new models of computer organization and architecture
- Evaluate architectures and the factors that shape their performance
